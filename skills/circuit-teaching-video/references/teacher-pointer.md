# Semantic teacher pointer

Choose attention from the explanation rather than from a fixed gesture quota. A writing pointer follows the actual reveal front at a safe offset. A frame highlights a meaningful region; an arrow explains a named relation. Do not let an arrow look like a new circuit wire. Give the audience one primary target at a time.

Retain the diagram and earlier derivation while adding new objects. Finish important writing before its read window. A stationary shot or an interval without a pointer is valid when it serves comprehension. Provider sentence timing does not justify word-level alignment claims.

Keep semantic targets and reveal geometry in world coordinates until the same camera transform projects them once. Check final-pixel clearance against visible glyph and diagram ink. Screen-fixed captions have a distinct coordinate space and reserved strip.

The public renderer reuses the production native-raster reveal grouping and actual front geometry. Foreground annotations are excluded from expanded content ink. Optional pale fills are underneath immutable content, not tinted rectangles over text. See [typography/layering](whiteboard-typography.md) for white-matte limits.

`scripts/check_teacher_pointer.py` audits its documented exported audit format; it does not automatically convert arbitrary renderer receipts or prove semantics. The integrated pipeline checks its own actual-frame geometry; independent video inspection is still required.
